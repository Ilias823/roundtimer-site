Round Timer · Support

Privacy Policy

Last updated 21 November 2026

Round Timer does not collect any data. It has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking, and it never sends anything about you over the internet. Everything it stores stays on your device.

What the app stores on your device

  • Settings and presets — your timer settings, saved timers and plans, and the order you keep them in.
  • Session history — when each session ran, what it was, how long it lasted, and, if you use Apple Health, a summary of the heart rate measured during it (zones, average, peak, recovery).

This data lives only in the app's own storage on your iPhone or iPad. It is included in your device's normal iCloud or computer backup, if you have backups turned on, under Apple's terms. You can remove your history at any time with Clear history in the History screen; deleting the app removes everything.

Apple Health

If you allow it, the app reads heart rate and active energy from Apple Health for the minutes a session ran, to draw that session's graph and summary. It never writes to Apple Health and never changes your Health data. Health data is not shared with anyone, not used for advertising, and not stored anywhere but your device. You can turn access off at any time in the Settings app under Health → Data Access & Devices.

Phone calls

The app checks whether a phone call is in progress so that it can pause a session when you take a call. It does not see who is calling, numbers, or any call details.

Purchases

The optional Unlimited Presets purchase is handled entirely by Apple through the App Store. The app learns only whether the purchase has been made. It never sees your payment details or Apple ID.

Children

The app collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date. If the app ever starts collecting data, this page will say so before it does.
