Common questions

Can I use it with gloves on?

Not the screen — boxing gloves can't operate a touchscreen. The app is built so you don't need to: set the session up bare-handed, press START, glove up, and follow the bells and voice. Nothing during a session needs a touch.

Does it keep running with the screen locked?

Yes. Bells and callouts keep playing with the screen off and alongside your music, which dips under each bell. If you don't hear them, check the volume and that your audio is going to the speaker you expect.

Why did my session pause?

Only a phone call pauses a session. The paused screen says why it paused. Press RESUME to carry on.

Why is there no heart rate on a session?

Heart rate comes from Apple Health, recorded by an Apple Watch, chest strap or similar during the session. Start a workout on your watch before you train, and allow Thairounds to read heart rate when asked. Watch data can take a few minutes to arrive, so open the session again a little later.

I bought Unlimited Presets on another device

Open Settings in the app and tap RESTORE.

How do I delete my data?

History → Clear history removes every logged session. Deleting the app removes everything. Nothing is stored anywhere else — see the privacy policy.
